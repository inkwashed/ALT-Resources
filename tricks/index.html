    Getting started

    Bag of Tricks keeps quick random classroom tools together in one place.

    1. 1
      Choose a mode

      Switch between Coin, Dice, and Cards using the tabs at the top.

    2. 2
      Run the randomizer

      Flip the coin, choose one or two dice and roll, or draw a card from the deck.

    3. 3
      Reset the cards

      Cards are not returned after a draw. Shuffle the deck to restore all 52 cards and clear the recent draws.
