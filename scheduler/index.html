Getting started

Set up the Scheduler once, then create correctly timed Google Calendar events in just a few clicks. Your information stays in this browser unless you export it.

  1. 1

    Add your schools and bell schedules

    Rename or add schools, enter their classes and locations, and adjust regular, irregular, and lunch times.

  2. 2

    Save your teaching dates

    Add dates for each school so the Target Date controls can quickly move between your scheduled school days.

  3. 3

    Back up or move your data

    Export a backup file periodically. Import it to restore your setup or move it to another browser or device.

Settings

CAUTION: Your schools, schedules, and settings are saved only in this browser/profile. They are not synced through your Google account. Export a backup periodically if you want to preserve your data or move it to another device or browser.
