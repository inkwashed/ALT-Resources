Spinner Wheel help

The wheel starts with numbers 1–24. Use Settings whenever you want to change how it works.

1. Choose the wheel mode

Use Numbers for an inclusive minimum-to-maximum range, with optional exclusions. Use Custom Choices for names, prompts, or other text entered one item per line.

2. Decide what happens after a spin

Selections can stay available for repeats or be removed temporarily for the current round. Removed choices return when you restore the round, change wheel entries, or reload the page.

3. Spin and announce the result

The selected choice is shown in full and announced for screen readers. Reduced-motion preferences replace the long spin with a brief transition.

Your settings and custom choices are saved only in this browser. Clearing site data can remove them; export a backup if the list is important.
